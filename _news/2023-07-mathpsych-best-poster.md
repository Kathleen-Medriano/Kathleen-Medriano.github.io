Won **Best Poster Award** at the MathPsych/ICCM/EMPG 2023 Conference for the poster "Bayesian hierarchical Ornstein-Uhlenbeck models for intervention designs."

**Reference:** Medriano, K., Oravecz, Z., & Vandekerckhove, J. (2023, July). Bayesian hierarchical Ornstein-Uhlenbeck models for intervention designs. Abstract published at MathPsych/ICCM/EMPG 2023. Via [mathpsych.org/presentation/1235](https://mathpsych.org/presentation/1235).
