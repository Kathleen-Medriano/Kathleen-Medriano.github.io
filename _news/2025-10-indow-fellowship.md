Received the **Tarow and Minako Indow Fellowship for Research Excellence**.

This prestigious fellowship is awarded annually to two graduate students in the Cognitive Sciences Department in recognition of exceptional research skills and productivity, as demonstrated during advancement to PhD candidacy.

**Awarded for research on:** "Methods for Time Series Data in Cognitive and Psychological Sciences" under the supervision of Dr. Joachim Vandekerckhove.
