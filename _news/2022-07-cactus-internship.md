Selected for the highly competitive [CaCTüS (Computational and Cognitive Theories Ünder Scrutiny) Internship program](https://www.projects.tuebingen.mpg.de/cactues-2022/) at the Max Planck Institute for Biological Cybernetics in Tübingen, Germany. The program has an acceptance rate of less than 2%.

Conducted research on generalization of learned structural knowledge across cognitive tasks using graph similarity in Dr. Peter Dayan's lab.
