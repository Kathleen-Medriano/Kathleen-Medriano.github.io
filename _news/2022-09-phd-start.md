Officially started PhD in Cognitive Sciences at the University of California, Irvine, working under the supervision of Dr. Joachim Vandekerckhove.

Research focus: Developing Bayesian hierarchical models for intervention designs in mobile health applications and creating statistical methods for time series data in cognitive science.
