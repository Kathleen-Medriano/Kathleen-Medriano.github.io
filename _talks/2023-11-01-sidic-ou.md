Extended OU model analysis with BayesFlow implementation for simulation-based inference. Explored intervention effectiveness through Bayes factors on linear contrasts, revealing habituation patterns in attractor and elasticity parameters. Investigated BayesFlow applicability for covariates, multidimensional variates, and response artifacts.
